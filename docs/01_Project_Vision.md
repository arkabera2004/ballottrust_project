# 1. Executive Summary

BallotTrust is an enterprise-grade Election Management Platform designed to digitize, secure, and simplify the complete election lifecycle.

Unlike traditional voting applications that focus only on casting votes, BallotTrust manages the entire election process—from election planning and candidate nomination to secure voting, result publication, auditing, reporting, and public transparency.

The platform is designed using modern software engineering principles with a modular architecture, making it scalable, maintainable, secure, and suitable for educational institutions, private organizations, professional bodies, and future government-level elections.

BallotTrust aims to replace fragmented election workflows with one unified, transparent, and auditable ecosystem.

# 4. Problem Statement

Many election processes continue to rely on manual or semi-digital workflows that introduce inefficiencies and security risks.

Common challenges include:

- Paper-based candidate nominations
- Manual voter verification
- Lack of centralized document management
- Limited transparency
- Weak auditing mechanisms
- Poor communication between stakeholders
- Inefficient election monitoring
- Difficult result publication
- Limited access to candidate information
- Inconsistent approval workflows

These issues increase administrative overhead and reduce confidence in election processes.

BallotTrust addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, secure, and workflow-driven election management platform.

# 7. Target Users

BallotTrust serves multiple categories of users.

## Internal Users

### Super Administrator

Responsible for overall platform administration, configuration, security, and maintenance.

### Election Commissioner

Responsible for planning, conducting, and managing elections.

### Verification Officer

Responsible for verifying candidates, voters, and submitted documents.

### Returning Officer

Responsible for operational election activities assigned during election execution.

### Auditor

Responsible for reviewing audit logs, monitoring compliance, and ensuring election integrity.

---

## External Users

### Candidate

Candidates manage their personal profile, upload required documents, publish manifestos, respond to verification requests, and monitor nomination status.

### Voter

Voters register for elections, review candidate profiles, compare manifestos, cast votes securely, verify vote receipts, and access election results.

---

## Public Users

Public users do not require authentication.

They can:

- View public elections
- Explore candidate profiles
- Read candidate manifestos
- View election statistics
- Access published election results
- Verify vote receipts using verification codes
- Download publicly available reports
